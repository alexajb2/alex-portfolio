Wildlife Camera-Trap Research

Computer vision for detecting Hume's Leaf Warblers in Himalayan camera-trap footage

Graduate Research Assistant · May – September 2026

Overview

As a graduate research assistant with Georgia Tech's Human Augmented Analytics Group (HAAG), I developed computer vision pipelines to detect and classify Hume's Leaf Warblers from Himalayan camera-trap footage. The goal is to support automated wildlife monitoring on annotation-limited ecological data.

My Contributions

  • Trained and evaluated object detection models, including YOLO, RT-DETR, DETR, and Swin Transformer-based architectures, on camera-trap imagery.
  • Benchmarked detection performance across 50,000+ annotated frames, including 13,000+ positive examples, to inform model selection.
  • Ran HPC experiments varying hyperparameters, data augmentation, and dataset splits to study performance with limited annotations.

Tech Stack

  • Python, PyTorch
  • YOLO, RT-DETR / DETR, Swin Transformer-based detectors
  • HPC experiment workflows, object detection, and model evaluation on ecological video
