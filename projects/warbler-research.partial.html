Wildlife Camera-Trap Research

Computer vision for detecting Hume's Leaf Warblers in Himalayan camera-trap footage

Overview

Graduate research assistant work with Georgia Tech's Human Augmented Analytics Group (HAAG), building computer vision pipelines to detect and classify Hume's Leaf Warblers from Himalayan camera-trap footage. The goal is to support automated wildlife monitoring on annotation-limited ecological data.

What I'm Working On

  • Training and evaluating object detection models — including YOLO, RT-DETR, DETR, and Swin Transformer-based architectures — to detect and classify the warblers.
  • Benchmarking detection performance across 50,000+ annotated frames (with 13,000+ positive examples) to inform model selection.
  • Exploring tracking and behavior-analysis methods to support automated wildlife monitoring.
  • Preparing a first-author journal manuscript on automated wildlife detection and behavior analysis (in progress, not yet submitted).

Tech Stack

  • Python, PyTorch
  • YOLO, RT-DETR / DETR, Swin Transformer-based detectors
  • Object detection, tracking, and behavior analysis on ecological video
